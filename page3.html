Statistics

Italy's resident population stands at approximately 58.9 million as of mid-2026, ranking it as the 25th most populous country globally. The nation faces a significant demographic shift, marked by a high median age of 48.6 years and a total fertility rate that has dipped to 1.14 children per woman. This natural population decline has been primarily stabilized by a net immigration influx of nearly 296,000 individuals over the past year. On the economic front, Italy boasts a nominal Gross Domestic Product (GDP) of roughly $2.74 trillion, maintaining its position as the eighth-largest economy in the world. Real GDP growth is projected at a modest 0.5% to 0.6% for 2026, supported by resilient export sectors and domestic manufacturing. Meanwhile, the national headline inflation rate softened slightly to 3.0% by mid-year, even as consumer markets navigate high energy prices and global supply chain pressures. The country's unemployment rate sits around 5.5%, but its public debt remains a structural challenge, hovering at 138.4% of the national GDP. Cultural and infrastructure metrics remain strong, with approximately 72.4% of Italians living in urban environments and the country hosting a world-leading 61 UNESCO World Heritage Sites to anchor its robust tourism sector.
